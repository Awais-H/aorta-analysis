1.5 mm pseudo-labelled check, commit 2d6e17b, 2026-09-13 05:55
Each fine case is resampled to 1.5 mm isotropic (CT linear, mask nearest neighbour, same physical frame) and the
coarse predictions are matched to the native predictions (pseudo-references) with the scorer at the 5 mm cutoff.
The native predictions are not ground truth: a branch 'lost' may be a native false positive and one 'gained' may be real.
This is a measurement of the resolution regime of the hidden set, not a tuning set (SPEC section 3).
